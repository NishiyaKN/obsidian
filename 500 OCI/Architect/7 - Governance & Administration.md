## Pricing
- All OCI regions have the same pricing
- No cost for data transfer between AD inside the same region
- Incoming traffic is free, outgoing traffic is lower than the competition
### PAYG (Pay as you go)
- Charged only for the resource consumed
- No upfront commitment
- No minimun service period
- Usaged metered
### Annual Universal Credits
- Commit to an annual pool of funds
- Significant savings
- Must use credits within 12 months
- Discounts based on size of deal and term of deal
- **There is also a Monthly Universal redit**
### BYOL (Bring your own license)
- For on-premises license
- Overall reduces cost
## Cost Management
### Budgets
- Can be compartment specific
- Sends notifications if cost is above a define threshold, is forecast to surpass or it actually surpasses the budget
### Cost Analysis
- Analyse past costs
### Usage Reports
- Usage of the entire tenancy
- Generate daily
- CSV file, can be exported
### Compartment Quotas
- Can set policies to:
	- limit the number of resources a compartment can use
	- remove access to a specific cloud resource in the compartment
