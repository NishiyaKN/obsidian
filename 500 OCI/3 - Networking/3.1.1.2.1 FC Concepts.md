### FastConnect location
- Data center where you can connect to OCI
- Usually has a Oracle Cage
	- In the cage there is Oracle Edge Servers
#### Oracle Edge
- Physical Device - handles Cross-Connect
- Logical Device - handles Virtual Circuit
### Metro Area
- Area with multiple FastConnect locations
### Network Service Providers
- **Oracle Partner**: integrated with Oracle in a FC location
- **Thrid-party provider**: no FastConnect
### Colocation
- When user equipment is deployed in a FC location
### Virtual Circuit
- The logical connection, runs over the physical connection
#### Public 
- On-prem to pulblic OCI services (Oracle Services Network)
- Don't need DRG
#### Private 
- On-prem to VCN
- Needs a DRG
### Cross-Connect
- The physical connection
#### Cross-Connection Group
- Used when need an increased bandwidth
### BGP Session
- Exchange of routing information between two different autonomous systems
### BFD - Bi-direction Forwarding Detection
- Verifies connectivity between devices
	- Detects failures
### LOA - Letter of Authorization
- Not applicable to Oracle Partners
- Applicable to third-party provider, needs to be given to them
- Applicable to colocation, needs to be given to the FC location technician
