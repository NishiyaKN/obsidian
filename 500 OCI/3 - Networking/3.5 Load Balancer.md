Reverse Proxy
- High availability and scalability
## Public LB
- Always sits inside a public subnet, as such has a public IP address
- Scope is a region
	- Requires either a regional subnet or at least 2 AD specific subnets
### Layer 7 LB
- Understand HTTP/S
- Flexible shape: defines minimum and maximum bandwidth
	- 10 mbps to 8gbps
- Dynamic shape: automatically scales bandwidth according to demand
- Content-Based Routing
	- Can inspect the package to route the package
### Layer 4 LB
- Network Load Balancer
- Works with TCP, UDP & ICMP
- Ultra-low latency, faster than Layer 7 LB
