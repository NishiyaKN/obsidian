## Lower Cost
- For large sequential I/O workloads (2 IOPS/GB)
	- Streaming
	- Data warehousing
## Balanced
- Random I/O workloads (60 IOPS/GB)
- 10 VPUs per GB/month
	- Boot disks
## Higher Performance
- Most I/O demanding workloads (75 IOPS/GB)
- 20 VPUs per GB/month
## Ultra High Performance
- Highest I/O demanding workloads (90-225 IOPS/GB)
	- Relational databases
- IOPS caps at 300,000 
- 30-120 VPUs per GB/month
## Auto-Tune 
### Performance Based
- Adjusts VPUs by demand
	- VPUs (Volume Performance Units) basically dictates how many IOPS/GB and throughput/GB
	- Must be at least 10 VPUs more than the default value
#### Performance Metrics
- Volume throttled operations
- Volume guaranteed VPUs/GB
- Volume guaranteed IOPS
- Volume guaranteed throughtput
### Detached Volume
- Change volume to Lower Cost when dettached
	- Saves money when the volume is not being used
- Returns to the previous setting when attached again
