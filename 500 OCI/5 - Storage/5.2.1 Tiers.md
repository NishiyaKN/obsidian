## Lower Cost
- For large sequential I/O workloads (2 IOPS/GB)
	- Streaming
	- Data warehousing
## Balanced
- Random I/O workloads (60 IOPS/GB)
	- Boot disks
## Higher Performance
- Most I/O demanding workloads (75 IOPS/GB)
## Ultra High Performance
- Highest I/O demanding workloads (90-225 IOPS/GB)
	- Relational databases
- IOPS caps at 300,000 
## Auto-Tune 
### Performance Based
- Adjusts VPUs by demand
	- VPUs (Volume Performance Units) basically dictates how many IOPS/GB and throughput/GB it can do per gigabyte of storage
### Detached Volume
- Change volume to Lower Cost when dettached
	- Saves money when the volume is not being used
- Returns to the previous setting when attached again
