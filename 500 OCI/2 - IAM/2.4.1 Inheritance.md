==Compartments inherit any policy from their parent compartment==
- Administrators group, from the default domain, has the policy to manage all resources in the tenancy
	- By inheritance, that extends to all compartment and sub-compartments too
# Attachment
- Write a policy once, can attach to different compartments
- Policies within a compartment can be changed by anyone allowed to manage policies in the compartment
	- To restrict control of a policy, you can attach it to a parent compartment so the child inherits it

	- In the tenancy example, it specifies the **path** to the target compartment (C)
		- Only those allowed to manage tenancy policies can change this policy
