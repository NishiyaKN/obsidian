
## Regions
- Geografical area
- Comprised of 1 or more AD
### AD (Availability Domain)
- Datacenter
- Isolated from each other
- Comprised of 3 FD
#### FD (Fault Domain)
- Infrastructure within an AD to provide anti-affinity
- Isolated from the other FD within the same AD
- Logical datacenters
