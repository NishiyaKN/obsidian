- Enables computers to process and understand human language (text and voice)
### Stages of NLP
1. **Tokenization**: breaks down **text** into smaller pieces called **tokens**
2. **Parse:** analyze the **grammatical** structure of the tokens, how they **relate** to each other
3. **Semantics:** try to understand the **meaning** behind the words (context, synonyms, intended meaning)
4. **Contextual Understanding:** uses **context** to create **meaning**, try to understand the implied meaning
5. **Statistical Inference:** uses probability to predict the next word
6. **Machine Learning:** learns from new inputs and improves
